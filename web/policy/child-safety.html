Pro Earn — Child Safety Standards

Pro Earn strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, and child sexual trafficking or exploitation.

Reporting

Users can report posts, comments, messages, and accounts from inside the app. Child-safety reports receive priority handling.

CSAM response

When Pro Earn obtains actual knowledge of CSAM or other CSAE content, we take appropriate action, including removal and reporting to the appropriate authority where required by applicable law.
