Account control · Privacy

Delete your ProSN account and data

You can request permanent deletion of your ProSN account using the secure verification process below. Enter the email address linked to your account. We will send you a secure sign-in link. Open that link on this page to verify account ownership, then confirm deletion. You can also delete your account in the app under Settings → Security → Delete Account.

After confirmation, the account deletion process removes the account profile, posts, comments, conversations, notifications, rewards records and user media stored by ProSN. Limited safety records may be anonymized and retained where necessary for abuse prevention or legal compliance. Deletion cannot be undone.
