ProSN Community Guidelines

Last updated: 28 November 2026

These Guidelines apply to profiles, posts, images, short videos, comments, messages, rooms and all other activity on ProSN. They are part of our Terms of Service.

1. Child safety and sexual exploitation

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation, CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of a minor, sextortion, child trafficking or attempts to arrange sexual contact with a minor are strictly prohibited.
  • Do not create, request, share, store or link to sexual content involving a minor or material that facilitates a child's exploitation.
  • Do not use ProSN to contact, manipulate, threaten or blackmail a child for sexual purposes.

2. Sexual content and exploitation

  • Pornography, explicit sexual acts, sexual solicitation and non-consensual intimate imagery are prohibited.
  • Do not share sexual content without the consent of the people depicted.

3. Harassment, hate and threats

  • Targeted harassment, bullying, stalking, repeated unwanted contact, coordinated abuse and credible threats are prohibited.
  • Hateful or dehumanizing attacks against protected groups are prohibited.
  • Do not encourage self-harm, violence or real-world harm against a person or group.

4. Violence, terrorism and dangerous wrongdoing

  • Graphic violence intended to shock, glorify violence or celebrate serious injury is prohibited.
  • Praise, recruitment, propaganda or operational instructions supporting terrorism or violent extremist activity are prohibited.
  • Do not threaten, plan, promote or instruct others to commit violence or serious crimes.

5. Fraud, privacy and platform abuse

  • Scams, phishing, impersonation, deceptive schemes, spam, fake engagement, bots and manipulation of rankings or rewards are prohibited.
  • Do not dox people or publish private, identifying or sensitive information without authorization.
  • Malware, malicious links, credential theft, unauthorized access and instructions intended to facilitate cyber abuse are prohibited.
  • Copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity and other rights violations are prohibited.
  • Do not use ProSN for illegal goods or services or to evade applicable law.

6. Reporting and blocking

Use the in-app report controls to report posts, comments, messages or accounts that may violate these Guidelines. You can block users to limit unwanted interactions. Provide accurate information when reporting; reports may contain content snapshots and identifiers needed for review.

7. Review and enforcement

Reports and credible safety signals may be reviewed. Actions may include content removal, reduced visibility, feature restrictions, warnings, suspension or termination. Severe child-safety concerns receive priority handling. We may preserve relevant records and report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authority where required by applicable law. Automated moderation may not detect every violation.

8. Appeals

If your content or account is restricted and you believe the decision was incorrect, contact support.prosn@gmail.com. Include your username and relevant decision details. We will consider appeals where appropriate and restore access if a restriction is found to be mistaken.

9. Safety contact

For child-safety concerns or urgent safety reports, contact support.prosn@gmail.com. See our Child Safety Standards.
