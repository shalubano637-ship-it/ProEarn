Account control · Privacy

Delete your ProSN account and data

Enter the email and password for your ProSN account. ProSN sends a fresh Firebase verification email for every website deletion request, even when Firebase already shows the address as verified. Open the newest link and return to this page; the deletion confirmation stays locked until the verification return is checked. Google sign-in users can continue deleting their account in the app under Settings → Security → Delete Account.

A verification link will be sent to your account email.

After confirmation, the account deletion process removes the account profile, posts, comments, conversations, notifications, rewards records and user media stored by ProSN. Limited safety records may be anonymized and retained where necessary for abuse prevention or legal compliance. Deletion cannot be undone.
