Delete your ProSN account and data

This page verifies that you own the account email before submitting a permanent deletion. You can also delete your account in the app under Settings → Security → Delete Account.

After verification, the account deletion process removes the account profile, posts, comments, conversations, notifications, rewards records and user media stored by ProSN. Limited safety records may be anonymized and retained where necessary for abuse prevention or legal compliance. Deletion cannot be undone.

Older media hosted by third-party services such as ImgBB may not be removable automatically. Contact support if you need help with legacy media cleanup.
