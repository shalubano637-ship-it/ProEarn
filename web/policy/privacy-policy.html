ProSN Privacy Policy

Terms of Service

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how ProSN processes information when you use the app and related services, including profiles, posts, short videos, comments, direct and room messaging, social interactions, virtual items, notifications and advertising.

1. Information we process

  • Account and identity information: Firebase user ID, email address, email-verification status, username, profile name, profile image, biography and account settings. Google Sign-In provides account identity information when you choose that sign-in method.
  • Content and communications: posts, images, captions, comments, profile media, messages and other content you submit or choose to report.
  • Social and feature activity: follows, follow requests, likes, blocks, shares, notifications, message activity, coin and gift activity, reward claims, pass or subscription entitlements and transaction records.
  • Safety and moderation information: reports, reported-content snapshots or identifiers, moderation signals, enforcement actions, appeals and information used to investigate abuse, fraud or policy violations.
  • Device and technical information: push-notification tokens, app and device details, diagnostic events, crash information, security logs and technical data needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.
  • Advertising and purchase information: advertising identifiers and ad-delivery or interaction signals processed by advertising providers; product, purchase, subscription and entitlement information returned by Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment-card processing.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; maintain profiles and settings; provide feeds, posts, comments, messaging, rooms, notifications and virtual-item features; process purchases and entitlements; respond to reports; detect spam, fraud and prohibited content; enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines; diagnose crashes and improve reliability; secure the service; display or measure advertisements; and meet legal obligations.

3. Service providers and sharing

Depending on the feature and configuration, ProSN uses:

  • Firebase Authentication: email/password and Google-linked sign-in, account identity, user ID and verification status.
  • Supabase: database and server-side function infrastructure for app data and backend operations. The app presents Firebase ID tokens to Supabase for authenticated requests; Supabase is not the primary sign-in identity provider.
  • Cloudflare: R2 media storage and delivery, network infrastructure and Workers AI media moderation.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: push-notification delivery and device notification tokens.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Sentry: crash, error and diagnostic reporting where enabled in the relevant build.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): advertising and rewarded-ad delivery.
  • Google Play Billing: purchase and subscription processing. ProSN receives product and transaction information needed to verify purchases and apply entitlements; Google Play handles payment-card processing.
  • Google Sign-In: identity authentication when you choose to sign in with Google.

Providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation and as needed to provide the relevant service. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse or secure ProSN. We do not sell personal information.

4. Posts, messages and moderation

Public or shared content may be visible according to the feature and your settings. Direct or room messages are available to relevant participants and may be reviewed when reported or when reasonably necessary to investigate safety or abuse. Uploaded media may be checked on-device and by server-side moderation before publication. Automated checks are not perfect and do not guarantee detection of every harmful item. Reports may include content snapshots and identifiers needed for review.

5. Advertising, virtual items and purchases

Ad providers may process advertising identifiers, device information and ad-delivery or interaction signals under their own policies and available device/account settings. Coins, gifts and rewards are in-app digital features with no cash value unless a feature expressly states otherwise. Google Play processes purchases and subscriptions; ProSN may process product IDs, purchase tokens, transaction status and entitlement information.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as it is needed to operate your account and provide the service, and for additional periods where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting or legal compliance. When deletion is completed, account and associated user data and ProSN-managed media are deleted through the deletion process. Limited safety or legal records may be anonymized or retained where necessary. Provider or protected backup copies may remain for their applicable operational or legal retention period before removal or overwrite.

To request deletion, use Settings → Security → Delete Account in the app or our public account-deletion page.

7. Security

We use authenticated access, access controls, database row-level security, server-side authorization, rate limits and monitoring. No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can update available profile and account settings, manage notification preferences, block users, control account or post privacy settings and request deletion using in-app controls. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or erasure of personal information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a privacy complaint. Contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Children and safety

ProSN strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, including CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors and sextortion. Users can report suspected violations inside the app. See our Child Safety Standards and Community Guidelines.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries where they or their infrastructure operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such processing and transfers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as ProSN's features, providers or legal obligations change. We will post the revised version here and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, account deletion or safety concerns, contact support.prosn@gmail.com.
