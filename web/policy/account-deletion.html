Delete your ProSN account and data

Enter the email address linked to your ProSN account. We will send you a secure sign-in link. Open that link on this page to verify account ownership, then confirm deletion. You can also delete your account in the app under Settings → Security → Delete Account.

After confirmation, the account deletion process removes the account profile, posts, comments, conversations, notifications, rewards records and user media stored by ProSN. Limited safety records may be anonymized and retained where necessary for abuse prevention or legal compliance. Deletion cannot be undone.

Older media hosted by third-party services such as ImgBB may not be removable automatically. Contact support if you need help with legacy media cleanup.
