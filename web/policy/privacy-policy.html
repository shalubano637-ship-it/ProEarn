ProSN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how ProSN processes information when you use the ProSN social platform, including its posts, short videos, comments, direct messages, rooms, coins, gifts, notifications, and advertising features.

1. Eligibility

ProSN is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. Children must not create or use an account. Users enter their date of birth during sign-in or registration. The date of birth is used only to check the minimum age and is not stored; ProSN stores only an age-eligibility confirmation. ProSN prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation and provides reporting and moderation processes described in our Child Safety Standards.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, username, profile name, profile image, biography, account settings, and email-verification status.
  • User content: posts, images, captions, comments, chat messages, room messages, voice messages, profile media, and content you choose to report.
  • Social and product activity: follows, likes, shares, message activity, blocks, hidden posts, notifications, coin and gift transactions, reward claims, and account preferences.
  • Device and technical information: push-notification token, app/device information, diagnostic events, crash reports, and information used to secure the service and prevent abuse.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers and ad interaction signals may be processed by advertising providers according to their own settings and policies.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; operate feeds, messaging, rooms and rewards; deliver notifications; process user reports; detect spam, fraud and prohibited content; maintain service reliability; comply with law; and display or measure advertisements.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate ProSN. Depending on the feature and configuration, these include:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database services, and server-side functions.
  • Cloudflare: media storage and delivery through R2, network services, and server-side image moderation through Workers AI.
  • Google Firebase: push-notification delivery and related messaging infrastructure.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): advertising and rewarded-ad delivery.
  • Sentry: application diagnostics and crash reporting where enabled in the build.
  • ImgBB: legacy or feature-specific image hosting where an older upload flow remains in use.

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers only as needed to provide, secure, and support the service, and may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users and the platform.

5. User content, messages and moderation

Content you publish may be visible to other users according to your account and post privacy settings. Direct messages are available to their conversation participants and may be reviewed when reported or when required to investigate safety or abuse. Uploaded images may be checked on-device and by server-side moderation before storage or publication. Reports may include content snapshots and identifiers needed to investigate a complaint.

6. Advertising and rewards

ProSN may show ads, including rewarded ads. Advertising providers may process advertising identifiers, device information and ad-delivery signals under their own privacy policies. Rewards from ads are in-app virtual items or feature benefits; they are not a promise of cash payment. Virtual coins and gifts have no cash value unless an expressly approved feature states otherwise.

7. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate security, safety, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution and legal purposes. We use authentication, access controls, row-level security, server-side authorization and other safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion from Settings → Security → Delete Account in the app or through our public account-deletion page. The external page verifies control of the account email before processing deletion. Account data and user media stored in ProSN-managed storage are deleted as part of the process. Limited safety or legal records may be anonymized or retained where necessary for abuse prevention, legal compliance or dispute resolution. Some third-party hosted legacy media, including older ImgBB uploads, may not be removable automatically through ProSN. In those cases additional provider-side handling may be required.

9. Children's safety

ProSN prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors and CSAM. See our Child Safety Standards and Community Guidelines.

10. Your choices

You can manage account privacy, messaging preferences, notifications, blocked users and account deletion through the relevant in-app settings. You can also control advertising personalization through your device and Google account settings, where available.

11. Contact

For privacy, account deletion, or safety questions, contact support.prosn@gmail.com.
