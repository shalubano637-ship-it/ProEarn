ProSN — Child Safety Standards

Age eligibility: ProSN is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. Children must not create or use an account. Users enter their date of birth during sign-in or registration. The date of birth is used only to check the minimum age and is not stored; ProSN stores only an age-eligibility confirmation.

ProSN strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, and child sexual trafficking or exploitation.

Reporting

Users can report posts, comments, messages, and accounts from inside the app. Child-safety reports receive priority handling.

CSAM response

When ProSN obtains actual knowledge of CSAM or other CSAE content, we take appropriate action, including removal and reporting to the appropriate authority where required by applicable law.
