ProSN Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 28 November 2026

ProSN has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These Standards apply to all users, content and features and form part of our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

1. Prohibited conduct

ProSN prohibits:

  • Creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, storing or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion, blackmail or attempts to arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Sexual exploitation or trafficking of a child or using the service to facilitate a child's abuse.
  • Links, instructions, offers or other material that facilitate access to or distribution of CSAM or child exploitation.
  • Threatening, coercing, impersonating or manipulating a child for sexual or exploitative purposes.

2. In-app reporting

Users can report suspected child-safety violations from within ProSN, including reports about posts, comments, messages and accounts. Include relevant account or content details when safe to do so. Do not download, copy or redistribute suspected CSAM to make a report. You can also contact the child-safety point of contact below.

3. Review and enforcement

Child-safety reports receive priority handling. ProSN reviews reports and credible safety signals and may remove content, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts, and take other appropriate protective action. We may preserve relevant information to support safety investigations and lawful reporting. Automated tools may assist review, but do not guarantee that every harmful item will be detected.

4. CSAM response and legal cooperation

When ProSN obtains actual knowledge of CSAM or other CSAE content, we take appropriate action, including removing or disabling access to the content, enforcing against the responsible account and reporting to the relevant authority where required by applicable law. We cooperate with valid law-enforcement requests and applicable child-safety reporting obligations.

5. Appeals and account action

Users may contact support to appeal an enforcement decision, except where doing so would compromise a safety investigation or legal obligation. An appeal does not guarantee restoration. Limited safety records may be retained where necessary to prevent repeat abuse, protect users or comply with law.

6. Child Safety Point of Contact

Designated child-safety contact: support.prosn@gmail.com. This contact receives child-safety concerns and communications from Google Play and coordinates review, enforcement and appropriate escalation.
